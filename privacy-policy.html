Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Introduction

At SmartHub ("we", "our", or "us"), reachable at https://smarthub.online, protecting the privacy of our visitors is one of our top priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by SmartHub and how we utilize and protect it.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at privacy@smarthub.online.

2. Client-Side Processing Pledge (No File Uploads)

SmartHub's image tools, PDF utilities, calculators, text analyzers, developer formatters, and converters are designed to operate entirely client-side within your web browser (via HTML5 Canvas, WebAssembly, and local JavaScript).

Your files, documents, and input text are NEVER transferred, uploaded, or stored on our servers. All computational workloads and modifications take place directly on your computer or mobile device. Once you close your browser tab or clear your session, your processed data is immediately released from memory.

3. Log Files and Standard Web Analytics

Like almost all standard web platforms, SmartHub follows standard logging procedures. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking aggregate user traffic, and gathering demographic information.

4. Cookies, Web Beacons & Google AdSense

SmartHub uses "cookies" to store information including visitors' preferences (such as light/dark theme preference) and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed. The information is used to optimize user experience by customizing web page content.

Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising:

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve advertisements on SmartHub.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to SmartHub and/or other websites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings at https://www.google.com/settings/ads or through the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page at www.aboutads.info.

5. CCPA & GDPR Privacy Rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users are entitled to rights including:

  • The right to know what personal data is collected and request deletion.
  • The right to opt-out of the sale of personal data (SmartHub never sells personal data).
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights.

6. Children's Information

SmartHub does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will promptly remove such information.

7. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
